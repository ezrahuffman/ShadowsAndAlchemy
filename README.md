# HELLO 👋

This is a game that I made for the 2024 Pirate Software game jam. 

## So what is the game?
The game is called Alchemy Assassin (yes, I know it is different from the repo). The goal of the game is to hide in the shadows and assasinate your target.

## Play it now 🕹️
You can play the game [here](https://leftovergames.itch.io/alchemy-assassin).

## Rules 📘 (also on itch.io)
In this adventure/puzzle game you play as an alchemist assassin who must transform ordinary objects into deadly weapons. You then must sneak around in the shadows (if you are seen in the light the guards will catch you), to avoid the guards. You can also eliminate the guards as long as they don't see you. Once you make your way to the back room, eliminate your target to win. 

## CONTROLS ⌨️ (also in help menu)
* e - interact
* wasd - move
* space - attack
* Being seen in the light will get you caught, and end the game.
* Eliminate the target in the back room to win.
